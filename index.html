    PDF warning

    SCANNED PDF DOCUMENTS WILL NOT WORK PROPERLY

    PDFs with real text work best. A scan or photo of a page has no text to read. Page numbers and headers are removed automatically.

          Settings

          You

          Name is used for your backup file, like Dan.Atom. Teacher mode turns off the mascot and needs the teacher password. Adult mode keeps the unlocks and hides school things. Atom drops by pops up on the home screen now and then.

          Home screen icon

          Pick one, then add Atom to your Home Screen from Safari. Already added? Remove it and add it again. Works on iPhone and iPad.

          Daily goal

          Your streak counts days you reach the goal. Words only count the first time you read them, going forward. Weekends don't count for or against your streak.

          Pause for breaks

          Days in a pause don't count for or against your streak. Adding or removing one needs the teacher password.
